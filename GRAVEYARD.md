# Graveyard

Tools that were once standard but are archived or unmaintained, with what to use instead. Back to the [list](README.md).

| Tool | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| [de4dot](https://github.com/de4dot/de4dot) | Archived Aug 2020 | ILSpy for reading; dnSpyEx for debugging. |
| [class-dump](https://github.com/nygard/class-dump) | No commits since 2022 | `ipsw class-dump`, or Hopper. |
| [Cloudflare har-sanitizer](https://github.com/cloudflare/har-sanitizer) | Archived Apr 2026 | A conservative jq redaction in [Recipes](README.md#-recipes), then a manual check. |
| [dex2jar](https://github.com/pxb1988/dex2jar) | No commits since Jul 2024 | JADX. |
